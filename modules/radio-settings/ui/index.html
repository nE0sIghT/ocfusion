Regional profile

The regional profile sets channel and transmit power limits for all radios. Select settings permitted at your location.

Saving does not restart Wi-Fi. Reboot the router through ASUS to apply or restore the profile.

Saved settings remain after USB removal or module disable. Restore the original settings before removal; restoration also requires a reboot.

Only profiles supported by every radio can be selected. Channel availability depends on the firmware, radio and channel width.

Channels and frequencies

2.4 GHz: channels 1-13 have center frequencies from 2412 to 2472 MHz. Channel 14 is 2484 MHz and requires specific radio and regional support.

5 GHz: channel center frequency is 5000 + channel number × 5 MHz. Channels 36-64 span 5180-5320 MHz; channels 100-165 span 5500-5825 MHz.

6 GHz: channels 1-233 have center frequencies from 5955 to 7115 MHz. Support for the upper band depends on the radio and regional profile.

These are channel center frequencies. Wider channels occupy more spectrum. Changing region does not remove hardware limits or DFS requirements.
